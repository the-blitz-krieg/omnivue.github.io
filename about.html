●   About Omni Vue AI

We built the platform we couldn't find.

Omni Vue AI exists because too many brands are still spending millions on advertising they can't measure, in channels they can't see, against competitors they can't track. We're fixing that.

02 · Our story Why we exist

The problem that wouldn't go away.

We watched too many marketing leaders walk into too many boardrooms with too little defensible data. - The origin of Omni Vue AI

Spend long enough in marketing and one fact becomes impossible to ignore: brand measurement has fallen decades behind brand spending.

Brands are pouring billions into sponsorships, paid media, and brand campaigns. But the tools they use to measure that spend were built for a world of cable TV, magazine ads, and text-based Twitter posts. That world has been replaced, by Reels, by streaming, by connected TV, by image-led influencer feeds, by video everywhere, and most measurement platforms are still pretending it hasn't.

We watched too many sponsorship deals get renewed on the strength of golf-day relationships rather than measured value. We watched agencies lose pitches they should have won because they couldn't prove what they'd delivered. We watched CMOs defend budgets with sentiment dashboards that read 15% of where their brand actually appeared.

So we built something different. A platform that measures brand exposure the way brand exposure actually exists in 2026, everywhere, in every format, in real time, with the rigour the spend deserves.

That's Omni Vue AI.

03 · Convictions What we believe

Four convictions. One platform.

01 / Decisions deserve real data

Marketing decisions deserve real data.

Multi-million-dollar bets shouldn't be made on sentiment scores and gut feel. They deserve the same data discipline as supply chain, finance, and product.

02 / Coverage gaps are accountability gaps

Coverage gaps are accountability gaps.

If you can only measure 15% of where your brand appears, you can't honestly claim to know what's working. Partial visibility produces partial truth.

03 / Speed is a feature

Speed is a feature.

Real-time data is the difference between adjusting a campaign and watching it fail. We optimise the platform for action, not archival reporting.

04 / Intelligence should be intuitive

Intelligence should be intuitive.

The best AI in the world is worthless if the people who need it can't use it. Omni Vue AI is built for marketers, not for machine learning engineers.

Nivin has been an entrepreneur and a marketer since the 8th grade. He started his first business, a window blinds manufacturing operation, at age 10, scaling it from his aunt's garage into a B2C and B2B operation working with large brands and premier architects. He has led multi-award-winning campaigns for clients across industries and has held senior leadership roles including Head of Digital Marketing at Muthoot Group-MLHS (a 130-year-old, US$820 million conglomerate) and Director of Digital Marketing at Ayruz, a data-driven digital agency based in Philadelphia.

Nivin also brings deep experience scouting and building technical project teams from scratch, managing high-profile talent, including some of the top 5% of engineers on StackOverflow, on SaaS and bespoke technology builds that have driven digital transformation for large brands and Fortune 500 companies in the US.

At Omni Vue AI, Nivin leads vision, strategy, and growth, with the conviction that the world's biggest brands deserve measurement tools built for the world as it actually is.
