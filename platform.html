Everything your brand does. Measured everywhere it goes.

Omni Vue AI is one platform with four engines — capture, measure, benchmark, and amplify — running across every channel your audience actually uses.

02 · Definition

Omni Vue AI is a full-spectrum brand intelligence SaaS platform. It uses proprietary computer vision, natural language processing, and machine learning to detect, measure, and value every appearance of a brand across the modern media ecosystem.

The platform ingests over a trillion data points per day from social media (text, image, and video), connected TV, linear television, OTT streaming services, and news media. It detects brand presence — logos, products, on-screen mentions, audio mentions, and contextual references — and translates each appearance into measured exposure value, ranked against competitors, campaigns, and historical baselines.

It replaces the patchwork of sentiment tools, manual clipping services, and unverifiable agency reports that most brands still live with.

03 · Engines Four engines, one truth

Capture. Measure. Benchmark. Amplify.

01

Capture

See everything. Miss nothing.

The Capture engine runs continuous detection across every channel in scope. Computer vision models trained on millions of branded media assets identify logos, products, packaging, jerseys, and signage — even when partially obscured, motion-blurred, or rendered at low resolution. Natural language models capture text mentions, audio references, and contextual associations.

What it covers

  • Social media: text, image, video, Stories, Reels, Shorts, live streams
  • Connected TV (CTV): smart TV and streaming ad inventory
  • Linear TV: national, regional, and local broadcast feeds
  • OTT platforms: ad-supported streaming, on-demand, podcast networks
  • News: online publications, broadcast segments, syndicated wires
02

Measure

Value every appearance. Defend every dollar.

The Measure engine converts every detected appearance into a quantified value. Exposure duration, on-screen size and prominence, audience reach, channel quality, sentiment, and competitive context combine into a single Exposure Value score — comparable across channels, campaigns, and competitors.

Core metrics

  • Exposure Value (EV) — total monetised brand exposure
  • Share of Voice (SOV) — your visibility versus competitors
  • Quality of Exposure (QoE) — prominence-weighted reach
  • Sentiment Index — content tone surrounding every appearance
  • Earned vs. Paid Split — what you bought versus what you earned
03

Benchmark

Know where you stand. Everywhere.

The Benchmark engine ranks your brand's performance against competitors, historical baselines, and industry norms — locally, nationally, and globally. Every metric is contextualised, so a number on a dashboard becomes a position in a market.

Benchmarks include

  • Competitor head-to-head across all channels
  • Industry index tracking
  • Geographic and market-tier performance
  • Campaign-over-campaign and year-over-year comparisons
  • Sponsorship-asset and placement-level rankings
04

Amplify

Act on what's working. Cut what isn't.

The Amplify engine closes the loop. Real-time alerts surface what matters the moment it happens. Recommendation modules identify which placements, sponsorships, creators, and channels are delivering the highest exposure value — and which are draining budget without return.

Capabilities

  • Custom alerts on metrics, thresholds, and competitor activity
  • Campaign-adjustment recommendations during live flights
  • Investment ranking by measured ROI
  • Audience-engagement and viral-moment detection
  • Exportable boardroom and renewal-meeting reports
