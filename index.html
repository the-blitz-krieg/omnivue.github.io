02 · Problem The data gap

You're spending millions. You're seeing thousands.

Every marketing leader knows the feeling. The campaign is live. The sponsorship is signed. The ad is running.

And the data on your screen covers maybe ten percent of where your brand actually shows up. Sentiment tools read text posts. They don't watch the video where your logo flashes for four seconds. They don't watch the broadcast that puts your jersey on screen for ninety minutes. They don't read the news segment, the OTT pre-roll, the influencer reel, the live stream, the highlight package, or the podcast clip.

They read text. That's it. So you're making multi-million-dollar decisions on a sliver of the picture, and calling it data.

Most brands measure less than 15% of where they actually appear. The rest is guesswork dressed as strategy. - The category gap, in one sentence

07 · The gap Old tools, new world

Why the old tools stopped being enough.

Sentiment platforms were built for a world where brand exposure meant text on Twitter. That world is gone. Today, your audience watches more than they read. They scroll Reels, stream live sport, binge series with mid-roll ads, catch news on TikTok, and see your brand in places no keyword search can find. Image and video have become the dominant formats. Connected TV has overtaken cable. And the tools most brands still pay for can't see any of it.
